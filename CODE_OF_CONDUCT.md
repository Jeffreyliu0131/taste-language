# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, safe, and useful. Contributors, maintainers, and reviewers are expected to treat one another with patience, directness, and good faith, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work and evidence, not the worth of the person.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, consent, image rights, and the project's non-claim boundaries.
- Stop sharing material when its source or permission is unclear.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and repository hygiene.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Uploading identifiable people, private places, personal data, or rights-unclear media.
- Deliberately misrepresenting prototype output as evidence about a person, a real place, or a sensitive trait.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a safety or conduct concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and protect the person reporting a concern.

For a conduct concern, contact the repository owner through a private contact method shown on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
