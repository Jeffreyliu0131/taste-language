## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-30

### Added

- Deterministic, local-first experience from context lock through training, freeze, holdout, language comparison, axis editing, separated results, reset, and JSON export.
- Fixture manifest, SHA-256, rights-field, schedule, dimension, and decode validation with fail-closed recovery.
- Corrupt-storage, bundle-mismatch, cross-tab/CAS, zero-axis, one-axis, low-margin, unsupported-edit, and adapter-failure handling.
- Desktop light, mobile dark, keyboard, reduced-motion, zoom/reflow, focus, touch-target, and accessibility checks.
- Public contribution, conduct, security, support, issue/PR, dependency-update, and CI governance.

### Evidence boundary

- The verdict remains **Validate first**. The prototype is not evidence of demand, real-place suitability, calibrated probability, research-grade blinding, or production readiness.
